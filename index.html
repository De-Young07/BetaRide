Execution Architecture

How the Negotiation Protocol Works

A disciplined three-step transaction flow designed to minimize street wait times and eliminate algorithmic surge gouging.

01

Route Definition & Fair Price Proposal

Passenger

The commuter selects their pickup point and destination from curated Ilorin landmarks. The system references the municipal benchmark index and suggests a base fair rate. The passenger inputs their proposed fare within the permissible corridor threshold.

02

Proximity Broadcast & Single Counter Option

Driver Pool

The offer is transmitted via lightweight WebSockets to the 10 nearest active operators within a 3-kilometer radius. A driver can confirm with one tap at the proposed fare, or issue a single structured counter-offer. Looped haggling is disabled to prevent distraction while driving.

03

Dispatch Lock & Direct Terminal Settlement

Fulfillment

The passenger selects their preferred driver based on rating, proximity, and counter-quote. The trip is logged on the dispatch registry. At drop-off, the passenger pays the driver directly via cash or mobile transfer. The platform takes 10% from the driver's pre-funded wallet.
